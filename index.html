About this tool

This pain-tracking tool was developed by Dr. Zainab Khan, a PhD holder in Geography whose research work spans environmental geography, climate change, spatial analysis and geospatial research.

However, her own experience of living with long-term musculoskeletal and inflammatory symptoms motivated her to develop a simple way for people living with autoimmune, inflammatory and arthritic conditions to document where pain occurs, monitor changes over time and maintain a structured record that can be exported or shared when needed.

The tool is intended to support personal symptom documentation and communication. It is not intended to diagnose disease or replace professional medical advice.

Pain Tracker stores records locally in your browser for this first version. Export your records regularly if you want a portable backup.
