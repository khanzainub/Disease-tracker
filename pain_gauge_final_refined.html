About this tool

Pain Gauge was created to make long-term pain documentation easier for people living with autoimmune, inflammatory, arthritic and persistent musculoskeletal conditions. It allows users to record where pain occurs, follow changes over time, and keep structured records that can be exported or shared when needed.

The tool was developed by Dr. Zainab Khan, inspired by her own long-term experience with musculoskeletal and inflammatory pain. She holds a PhD in Geography and has a scientific research background spanning environmental monitoring, climate change, hazards, spatial analysis and geospatial research.

Pain Gauge is intended to support personal symptom documentation and communication. It is not intended to diagnose disease or replace professional medical advice.
